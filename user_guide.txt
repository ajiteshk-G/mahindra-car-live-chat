Avatar  reference  image  instruction  To  ensure  the  highest  accuracy  for  your  digital  Avatar,  please  provide  a  reference  photo  that  
strictly

 Requirement  Specification  
Quality  High-resolution  (>=720p),  sharp  focus,  no  blur,  no  artifacts  
Framing  1.  Close-up  framing  (focused  primarily  on  head  and  shoulders)  2.  Exclude  the  lower  body  and  hands  3.  Ensure  the  backdrop  is  simple,  devoid  of  distinct  items  or  additional  individuals.  
Image  Size  9:16  (Portrait)  
Expression  Neutral  /  No  emotion  
Viewpoint  Direct,  clear  front-facing,  avoid  tilted  head  
Safety  Avoid  images  containing  celebrities,  children,  or  any  offensive  content.  
Framing  &  Zoom  (The  "Bust"  Shot)  
●  Zoom  In:  The  subject's  head  and  shoulders  should  occupy  >60%  of  the  frame .  ●  Avoid  Hands:  Ensure  hands,  arms,  or  any  objects  (phones,  microphones,  etc.)  are  
completely

of

.
 
 ●  Simple  background:  Ensure  the  backdrop  is  uncomplicated,  devoid  of  distinct  items  or

 ●  Ratio:  Use  a  standard  9:16  (Portrait)  aspect  ratio.  
Positioning  &  Perspective  
●  Clear  Front  Face:  The  subject  must  look  directly  into  the  camera  lens .   
Expression  &  Clarity  
●  Neutral  Face:  No  smiling,  squinting,  or  visible  teeth.  Maintain  a  relaxed,  "resting"  facial

 ●  High  Fidelity:  The  image  must  be  sharp  and  high-resolution.  Avoid  compressed,
